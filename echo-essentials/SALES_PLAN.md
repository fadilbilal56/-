# Echo Essentials: how to sell it

## 1. What you have
| File | Format | Role in the funnel |
|---|---|---|
| `1_Echo_Essentials_eBook.pdf` | 21 pages | Core product |
| `2_Echo_Essentials_Teaching_Slides.pptx` | 24 slides + speaker notes | Teaching licence for programme directors and educators |
| `3_Echo_Essentials_Cards_and_MCQs.pdf` | 9 pages | Revision kit and lead magnet |
| `packaging/` | JPG covers + bundle mockup | Store listing and social images |
| `landing/index.html` | Responsive sales page + free sample PDF | Where buyers land; the free sample is the email lead magnet |

## 2. Before you sell (do these first)
1. Replace `Dr. [Your Name]` in `build/v2/brand.py`, run `build/v2/build_all.sh`, and set author, email, prices and checkout links in `CONFIG` inside `landing/index.html` (see README).
2. **Verify every threshold against the full guideline texts** (especially 2025 ESC/EACTS class of recommendation wording and the ASE 2025 diastology algorithm). The text was written from the published criteria, but full guideline text could not be opened while building. Do a final read as the named author: your reputation is the product.
3. Add a medical-education disclaimer on the sales page (already inside the files). Do not reproduce copyrighted figures from guidelines; make your own images or request permission.
4. Add 3-5 of your own anonymised echo clips or still images (no patient identifiers, with consent/policy compliance). Images from real cases will lift perceived value more than any design.

## 3. Pricing (starting point, test it)
| Tier | Contents | Price idea |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Cards + MCQs | USD 9-12 (also free sample) |
| Core | eBook | USD 19-29 |
| Educator | Slides with teaching licence (one department) | USD 49-79 |
| Complete bundle | All three | USD 59-89 |
| Institution licence | Department / programme, multiple users | USD 199-399 |

Use a launch discount (30% for 7 days) and a founders' price that rises as you add chapters.

## 4. Where to sell
- **Own page + Stripe/Payhip/Gumroad**: fastest start, instant delivery, you own the customer list.
- **A second storefront for reach**: list the same files on one more established marketplace so buyers can find you by search, not only by your own links.
- **Telegram / WhatsApp Business channel** for echo fellows: free MCQ of the week, link to buy.
- **Direct institutional sales**: email programme directors and echo lab heads with the Educator tier.
- **Society and course partnerships**: offer to bundle with a workshop, journal club or board-review course.

## 5. Funnel in four steps
1. **Free hook**: 5 sample MCQs and the AS pocket card (PDF) in exchange for an email.
2. **Teach publicly**: short LinkedIn/X/Instagram carousels from the book: "Critical AS: what the numbers really are", "Two types of low-flow AS in 60 seconds", "The 5-line AS report".
3. **Email sequence (5 mails)**: AS error of the week, low-flow quiz, TR new grades, bundle offer, last-day reminder.
4. **Offer**: bundle link with a 7-day launch price and a money-back guarantee (14 days).

## 6. Content calendar (first 30 days)
- Week 1: AS grading carousel; free AS card; announce book.
- Week 2: low-flow case quiz (poll), then answer with explanation.
- Week 3: live webinar or recorded 30-min lecture to fellows using the slides; offer bundle at the end.
- Week 4: testimonials from first buyers; institution outreach.

## 7. Trust signals that sell
- Your name, title, institution, years of echo experience on page one.
- "Aligned to ASE/EACVI and 2025 ESC/EACTS" with version dates.
- A visible version number and free updates for 12 months.
- Reviews from 5-10 fellows before public launch (give free copies in exchange for honest feedback).

## 8. Protect the product
- Watermark each copy with the buyer's name/email (Gumroad and Payhip support this for PDFs).
- Single-user licence for the book; separate licence for the slides.
- Keep an update log; send buyers each new version (builds loyalty and reviews).
