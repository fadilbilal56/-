Equations

AVA = LVOT area × VTILVOT / VTIAV.   DVI = VTILVOT / VTIAV.   SVi = LVOT area × VTILVOT / BSA.   Low flow: SVi below 35 mL/m².

Critical AS

Adults: Vmax 5.0 m/s or more and / or mean gradient 60 mmHg or more, usually AVA below 0.6 cm² (ACC/AHA: very severe). No single universal definition, so report the numbers. Neonatal critical AS is a separate, duct-dependent condition.

Before you grade

  • Several windows and a dedicated non-imaging probe. Misalignment always underestimates.
  • Blood pressure controlled at the time of the study. Average five beats in atrial fibrillation.
  • LVOT diameter: squared in the formula. Cross-check with 3D or CT when in doubt, and look at DVI.

Dobutamine stress echo

Start 2.5 to 5, up to 20 µg/kg/min. Flow reserve = stroke volume rise of 20% or more. True-severe: AVA stays 1.0 or less with Vmax 4 m/s or more. Pseudo-severe: AVA rises above about 1.0 to 1.2 and the gradient stays low. No flow reserve: use CT calcium; benefit from valve replacement persists.

TR beyond severe. Massive: VC 14 to 20 mm, EROA 60 to 79 mm², RVol 60 to 74 mL. Torrential: VC 21 mm or more, EROA 80 mm² or more, RVol 75 mL or more.

Secondary MR. Severe in ESC practice at EROA 0.20 cm² or more or RVol 30 mL or more (elliptical orifice). COAPT enrolled EROA above 0.30 cm².

Mitral stenosis. Clinically significant at MVA 1.5 cm² or less; PHT 150 ms or more; very severe MVA 1.0 or less, PHT 220 or more. MVA = 220 / PHT. Wilkins 8 or less favours balloon commissurotomy; exclude left atrial thrombus and more than mild MR.

Triggers for surgery. AR: symptoms; ESC LVEF 50% or less, ACC/AHA 55% or less; LVESD above 50 mm. Primary MR: symptoms; LVESD 40 mm or more; LVEF 60% or less; new AF; PASP above 50 mmHg; LAVI 60 mL/m² or more.

Raised LAP: septal e′ below 7, lateral e′ below 10, average E/e′ above 14, TR above 2.8 m/s, LAVI above 34. In AF: septal E/e′ 11 or more, DT 160 ms or less. Use the ASE 2025 algorithm for grading and for HFpEF.

RV: TAPSE below 17 mm, S′ below 9.5 cm/s, FAC below 35%, free-wall strain less negative than about −20%, basal diameter above 41 mm, RA area above 18 cm².

PASP = 4V² + RAP (3, 8 or 15 mmHg). PH probability: TR 2.8 or less is low; 2.9 to 3.4 is intermediate; above 3.4 is high; other PH signs raise a category.
